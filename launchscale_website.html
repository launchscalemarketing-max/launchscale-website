Most B2B startups hit a revenue ceiling around $1M–$5M ARR because closing enterprise deals still relies entirely on the founder's intuition.

01

Founder Bottleneck

Deals stagnate whenever you step out of sales meetings because pitch knowledge isn't documented or repeatable.

02
